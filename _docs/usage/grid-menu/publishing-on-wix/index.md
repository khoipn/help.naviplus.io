# Grid Menu — Publish on Wix

Navi+ runs on Wix as a **native Wix App Market app**. A **Grid Menu** is a section menu — a block of tappable tiles you place inside a page or attach to your navigation. On Wix you don't write any CSS selectors: Navi+ **auto-fills the placement selectors for you**.

> Other platforms: see [Publish on Shopify]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/usage/grid-menu/publishing-on-shopify/), [Publish on WordPress]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/usage/grid-menu/publish-on-wordpress/), or [Squarespace / Webflow / others]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/usage/grid-menu/publish-on-wordpress-woocommerce-wix-others/).

---

## Steps

1. **Install Navi+ from the [Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market)** — it opens inside your Wix dashboard, already connected to your site.
2. **Build your Grid Menu** — see [Grid Menu — How to use]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/usage/grid-menu/how-to-use/).
3. **Choose placement and publish** — pick where the grid should appear (insert at a target, or attach to your navigation), then publish (status: Published).

Navi+ embeds itself through Wix's Embedded Script and applies the placement automatically — no code, no theme edit.

---

## Placement is automatic

Because a Grid Menu is placed at a spot on your site, Navi+ needs to know where. Wix sites share the same underlying structure (the "Thunderbolt" runtime), so **Navi+ already knows your header and main content areas** and fills the placement selectors in for you. Pick **insert** or **replace** in the publish panel — there is no CSS to write.
